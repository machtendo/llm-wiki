## Overview

This knowledge base serves as a collaborative workspace for human-agent knowledge curation. Sources are ingested from `raw_sources/` and synthesized into structured concepts, entities, computations, and playbooks in `wiki/`.

## Quick Start

1. **Ingest**: Drop a new source into `raw_sources/` and request ingestion
2. **Query**: Ask questions; answers draw from indexed wiki pages
3. **Lint**: Periodically request a health check to find gaps and orphans

For full operational guidelines, see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
